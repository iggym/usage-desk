05 / A healthier relationship with the headlines

Three numbers we won’t invent.

Absence of disclosure is a finding. Unknown is not zero.

🔎

A total “agent user” count

No consistent, deduplicated global figure is established by this source set. ChatGPT and Gemini reach cannot be added together or relabeled as autonomous-agent adoption.

🧾

A universal agent market size

Model revenue, agent-product run-rate, funding and SaaS seats have different scopes. Claude Code’s reported run-rate is a product signal—not the size of the whole market. [4]

🫥

Standalone Muse or Dots users

The reviewed launch sources don’t disclose separate active-user counts. We won’t borrow the parent platform’s reach, infer paid seats or manufacture a growth curve. [2][1]
