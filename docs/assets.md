The fifteen `builder-*.jpg` images and three `future-*.jpg` images are AI-generated visualisations. Each is captioned in the interface as an AI concept and is never presented as a portrait, a product photograph or documentary evidence. People are represented by generated still-life scenes chosen for their work, never by likenesses; the initials badge is only a fallback when a scene is missing.

The existing repository's MIT [license](../LICENSE) is retained. No new provenance claim is made for the inherited images beyond their presence in the original repository.

## Brand marks

SVG paths come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. A copy of the license is included at [`assets/logos/LICENSE.md`](../assets/logos/LICENSE.md).

- Simple Icons **16.35.0**: Anthropic, Claude, CrewAI, Cursor, GitHub, GitHub Copilot, Google, Google Gemini, Hugging Face, LangChain, Meta, n8n, Perplexity, Replit, X and Zapier.
- Simple Icons **13.21.0**: OpenAI and Salesforce (not present in the newer package).

Brand marks identify the corresponding products/organizations editorially. They do not imply sponsorship or endorsement. CC0 does **not** grant trademark rights. Organizations without a supplied local brand mark use a text initial rather than a fabricated official logo.
